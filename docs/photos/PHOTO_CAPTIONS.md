# Apparatus photographs

Twenty photographs of the experimental system, taken during the campaign at the
Department of Nuclear Energy, Universidade Federal de Pernambuco. They document
the apparatus described in the README and in the associated manuscript. Images
were reduced to a maximum dimension of 2400 px for deposit; originals are held by
the authors and available on request.
